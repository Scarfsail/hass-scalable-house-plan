# Getting started

Three steps take you from nothing to a working plan:

1. [Install](install.md) the card through HACS.
2. [Build your first plan](first-plan.md): a picture of your floor plan, its size and one room.
3. Use [the visual editor](visual-editor.md) to outline the remaining rooms and drop elements onto them.

If you'd like to start from something complete, the [demo](../demo.md) gives you the Willow Lane picture and its full configuration.
